# Practical validation — feedback guide (untested until run)

Show the live app to roughly three Nigerian consumers and two retailers/distributors. Do not contact anyone on the owner's behalf from the repo; this guide is for the owner to run. No interviews, partnerships or adoption are claimed until this is actually done.

## Session plan (15 minutes each)
1. Give no explanation first. Ask them to check the "Seller says it was returned" sample and say aloud what they think the result means.
2. Show the legitimate-return sample. Ask what changed their reading.
3. Ask the questions below; write answers in the template verbatim where possible.

## Questions
Consumers:
- Would this result change your decision to buy? What would you do next, in the shop, with this next step?
- Is the next action practical (who would you ask, what would you ask for)?
- What would make you trust or distrust the records shown?

Retailers/distributors:
- Who could supply trustworthy records like these, and at what point in your current workflow?
- What would motivate you to participate (record sales/returns this way)? What would stop you?
- What would this cost you in time per sale/return, honestly?

Adoption hypothesis being tested: one participating manufacturer onboarding a limited retailer network first. Treat answers as evidence for or against it, not confirmation.
